The Intro Course is a ten-week crash course in NeuroAI. Each week pairs a lecture with a unit: a reading from our course textbook, the [i2 Grimoire](https://grimoire.uw-i2.org), synthesis questions, and a project. Units have a technical track and a literacy track, so you can take the course with or without a programming background. Along the way you'll pick up PyTorch, Jupyter notebooks, LaTeX, and the habit of teaching yourself.

All of the [course materials](/intro-course) are open, so you can also work through them on your own.
