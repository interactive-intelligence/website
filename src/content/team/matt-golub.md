Matt Golub, PhD, is an Assistant Professor in the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Washington. He directs the Systems Neuroscience & AI Lab (SNAIL), which builds computational models at the intersection of neuroscience, machine learning, and neuroengineering to understand how neural population activity drives movement, decision-making, and learning.
