The application is due on December 22nd, 2026 at 11:59 PM. It includes a few short essay questions, so we recommend starting early.

We'll teach you how to start, conduct, and write up research, with the goal of contributing to a finished project. Meetings are currently planned for Fridays, but that may change based on availability.

You can read more on the [Research Room page](/initiatives/research-room) and at [research.uw-i2.org](https://research.uw-i2.org/).
