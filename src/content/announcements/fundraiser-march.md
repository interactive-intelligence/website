We're partnering with Cafe Allegro for a casual fundraiser night on **Saturday March 22, 6-9pm**.

Mention i2 at the counter and Allegro will donate a portion of your order back to us — which goes directly toward speaker honoraria, food at events, and travel for members presenting at conferences.

From 7-8pm, three current Fellows will give 5-minute lightning talks on what they've been working on this quarter. Show up, drink coffee, hear about cool research.
