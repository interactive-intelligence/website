Fellows is i2's most intensive program — a two-quarter commitment to a single research direction, paired with a faculty mentor and a small cohort of peers working on adjacent questions.

This cycle we're recruiting for projects across:

- mechanistic interpretability of vision-language models
- neural population geometry and representation learning
- predictive coding and active inference
- comparative architectures between transformers and cortical microcircuits

You don't need prior research experience, but you do need to be ready to put in real hours. Read the program description on the [Fellows page](/initiatives/fellows) before applying.
