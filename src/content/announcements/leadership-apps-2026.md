i2 is run entirely by students, and most of what makes the org work — the Intro Course, Fellows, Research Room, Talks — is owned by a specific person who decided to take it on.

For 2026-2027 we're recruiting:

- **Intro Course lead** — curriculum, recruitment, weekly instruction
- **Research Room coordinator** — team formation, mentor matching, demo days
- **Outreach lead** — partnerships with other UW orgs, external comms, recruiting

If you've been in i2 for at least a quarter and want a bigger role, this is the path. Time commitment is real (5-8 hrs/wk) but flexible.
