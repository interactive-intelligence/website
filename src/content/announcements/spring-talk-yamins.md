Daniel Yamins (Assistant Professor of Psychology and Computer Science at Stanford, and faculty at the Wu Tsai Neurosciences Institute) will give an i2 Talk on **Wednesday May 28th at 5:30pm in CSE2 G10**.

His lab studies how goal-driven training of deep networks produces representations that match neural recordings across the ventral stream — and what the remaining gaps tell us about how biological visual systems actually work.

Pizza will be provided. Open to all UW students; no background in neuroscience or ML required.
