---
title: Introducing DSI — our new directed study initiative
date: 2026-01-13
summary: We've launched DSI (Directed Study Initiative), a structured self-study program for members who want to go deeper on a specific topic than the Intro Course allows.
---

After two cycles of running it informally, we're making DSI an official i2 initiative starting Winter 2026.

DSI pairs small groups (2-4 people) with a curated reading list on a focused topic — past examples include *NeuroAI foundations*, *circuit-level interpretability*, and *predictive processing*. Groups meet weekly to discuss, and produce a writeup or short talk at the end of the quarter.

It's lighter-weight than Fellows but more structured than Research Room. Good fit for people who learn best by reading and talking, not by building. See the [DSI page](/initiatives/dsi) for the current quarter's topic list.
