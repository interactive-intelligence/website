## homepage main description

i2 is UW's student-led NeuroAI education and research organization. We aim to create a community of project-driven, outcome-oriented students interested in the intersections of artificial intelligence, neuroscience, and philosophy. We are interested in how machines can learn like humans.

## about page main description

interactive intelligence (i2) is the University of Washington's student-led NeuroAI education and research organization. We're a community of students learning, building, and researching at the intersection of artificial intelligence and neuroscience. Across our programs, members work on topics spanning deep learning, computational neuroscience, mechanistic interpretability, and the broader question of what intelligence is and how to build it.

### intro course (initiatives page)

A comprehensive introduction to the fundamentals of machine learning and neuroscience. Perfect for beginners looking to understand how biological and artificial intelligence systems work. Hands-on projects and weekly lectures designed to build a strong foundation.

### data science initiative (initiatives page)

A project-based initiative where members collaborate on real-world data science problems. Teams work on end-to-end pipelines — from data collection and cleaning to modeling and presentation — guided by experienced mentors.

### fellows (homepage)

A selective program for advanced students pursuing independent research at the intersection of AI and neuroscience.

### fellows (initiatives page)

A selective program for advanced students pursuing independent research at the intersection of AI and neuroscience. Fellows receive funding, mentorship, and a dedicated workspace to develop their own projects over the academic year.

### research room (homepage)

A collaborative space where small teams tackle open research questions in NeuroAI.

### research room (initiatives page)

A collaborative space where small teams tackle open research questions in NeuroAI. Each team is paired with a faculty advisor and works toward publishable results, with the goal of contributing to the broader scientific community.

### talks (homepage)

A speaker series featuring researchers, industry experts, and students presenting their work in AI, neuroscience, and adjacent fields.

### talks (initiatives page)

A speaker series featuring researchers, industry experts, and students presenting their work in AI, neuroscience, and adjacent fields. Talks are open to all and include time for discussion afterward.
